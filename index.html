About Us

Jazzmin Jazz Band is an 8-piece jazz ensemble based in Brisbane (Meanjin) and Gold Coast (Yugambeh) city. A group of university friends, Jazzmin brings a fun and energetic performance of everything from pop, to jazz, to funk, and swing. Starting in 2023, Jazzmin's original goal was for first year univeristy students to continue to pursue the training and education they had developed throughout high school.

The band's unusual lineup of alto saxophone, tenor saxophone 1 & 2, bass clarinet, keys, double bass, drums and vocals allows for a unique sound that is both versatile and transportable. The band creates their own arrangements of popular songs. Most recently, members of the band have been working with Griffith Film University to perform an original composition by our tenor saxophonist for their upcoming film, "Cadenza".

Jazzmin has performed at a variety of events, including private birthday and holiday parties, film studios, restaurants, and market events. We are always looking for new opportunities to perform and share our music with the community. If you are interested in booking Jazzmin for an event, please contact us through our social media routes.
